    Directions

    1. You get two popular snacks or drinks, usually from different countries. The game tells you how many flavors they share.
    2. Guess flavors they've both been sold in. If they share more than one, find them all, up to 3. Pick from the suggestions; spellings like "green tea" or "chilli lime" are understood.
    3. Each round is worth 1,000 points. Every wrong guess costs 125. Six misses ends the round, and you keep points for the flavors you found.
    4. Small typos are fine: "chese", "carmel" or "lime chili" are understood.
    5. Broad flavors count: a Habanero or Flamin' Hot snack matches "Hot & Spicy", and Nacho Cheese matches "Cheese".
    6. A wrong guess still shows a ✓ for any item that has that flavor, and adds it to that item's card as a clue.
    7. Hints: the flavor family of one you haven't found, then its first letter. They normally unlock after 2 and 4 misses.
    8. The game learns from how people play. Each Daily goes Easy → Medium → Hard: round 1 uses everyday flavors, round 3 needs a less common one. Puzzles get easier or harder over time based on results, and Endless adjusts to your own recent results.
    9. Daily is 3 rounds, for a best score of 3,000. New pairs every midnight, your time.
    10. Challenge is one daily round with three snacks or drinks. Endless deals new pairs or trios forever, just for fun: no points and no limit on guesses.

    Flavors count if they're part of the product's current range, and blends count toward each named flavor (Strawberry Lime counts as Strawberry and as Lime).
